3. Your information and backups

You control the entries and profile information you add. Core use is local to your device. Optional cloud sync requires Google sign-in and your confirmation; the Privacy Policy explains what is sent to Supabase. You are responsible for keeping your device and account secure.

Manual backup files are JSON and are not encrypted by the app. Restoring a backup may replace existing ledger data and related local backup sections; review the in-app confirmation carefully. Keep exported files private and retain your own safe copy.

4. Accounts and cloud service

Google sign-in is optional and used for cloud backup/sync. You must be authorized to use the Google account you choose. Cloud features depend on Google, Supabase, connectivity, and service availability. Signing out does not delete your cloud account or synced records. You can permanently delete the cloud account and synced records from Profile → Cloud Backup → Delete cloud account, or use the external instructions at https://keshab1997.github.io/khoroch-diary/account-deletion.html. This leaves the local ledger on the device; exported backup files must be deleted separately.
