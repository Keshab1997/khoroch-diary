## English listing

**Short description (80-character limit):**
> Track daily income and expenses in a simple, offline-first personal khata.

**Full description:**
> Keep your personal khata simple with Khoroch Diary.
>
> Record income and expenses, organize entries by category, manage multiple khatas, and check balances in one place. Your ledger works offline and does not require an account.
>
> **Keep track of everyday money**
> • Add income and expense entries with an amount, category, date, and optional description.
> • View balances and recent activity across your khatas.
> • Use the calculator and cash note counter for quick everyday calculations.
>
> **Your data, your choice**
> • Core ledger data is stored locally on your device.
> • Optional Google sign-in enables cloud backup and sync across devices.
> • Export or restore an offline JSON backup from Profile. Backup files are not encrypted by the app; store them somewhere private.
>
> Cloud backup is optional. See the Privacy Policy for details about account information and data sent to Supabase when you enable sync.
>
> Khoroch Diary is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide banking, payments, money transfers, loans, or investment advice.
